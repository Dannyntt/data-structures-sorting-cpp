Notes:
  - QuickSort uses median-of-three pivot (iterative stack) to avoid
    worst-case O(n²) on already-sorted or nearly-sorted data.
  - HeapSort guarantees O(n log n) but has poor cache locality
    due to non-sequential memory access patterns.
  - AVL Tree time includes both insertion (w/ rotations) and inorder
    traversal. Higher constant factor due to pointer overhead and
    heap allocations per node.
  - Memory: vector-based sorts reuse the same array in-place;
    AVL allocates one node (~56 bytes) per unique word.
